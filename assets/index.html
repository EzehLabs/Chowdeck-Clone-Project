FAQs.

What is Chowdeck?

Chowdeck is a food delivery platform that connects you with great restaurants and local food sellers. Choose what you're craving and we'll bring it to you.

How do I place an order?

Download the Chowdeck app, choose your delivery location, pick a restaurant and add your favourites to cart. We'll take it from there.

Which locations do you deliver to?

We deliver across a growing number of neighbourhoods. Enter your address in the app to see restaurants serving your area.

How can I track my order?

Follow your order in real time in the app, from the restaurant accepting it to your rider arriving at your door.

Can I become a vendor or rider?

Yes! We're always excited to welcome new restaurant partners and riders. Get in touch through the contact links below to join our network.

✦

What's Chowdeck?

Chowdeck is a technology company that provides a simple way to order food and groceries. We're passionate about making it easier for you to enjoy great meals from your favourite restaurants and vendors.
